Christie Cheney

Fearless, compassionate, and tough as hell. Known as CLee by her friends and family, Christie is a force to be reckoned with. She is a mentor, counselor, friend, daughter, sister, wife, mother, and all-around badass yoga instructor.

“Life is a journey that should be traveled well. And along the way, try to find something that sets your soul on fire — even if it scares you.”
— Christie Cheney
