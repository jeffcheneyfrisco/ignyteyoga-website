Studio procedures

A few agreements that keep class fair and the room calm.

  1. Reserve your space. Spots are limited. Book on the schedule so we can hold a place for you, and so someone else can have it if your plans change.
  2. Arrive early. Doors lock 10 minutes after class starts. Come with a little time to spare so you can get in, settle, and let your body meet the heat. Once the door is locked, class stays uninterrupted.
  3. A no-show or late cancellation is $15. If you can’t make it, cancel at least 12 hours ahead so someone else can take the spot. Inside that window, or if you don’t show, the fee is $15 on the card on file. If you’re having trouble cancelling, email hello@ignyteyoga.com and we’ll help.
  4. Help us keep the studio beautiful. No shoes in the study space.
  5. Leaving a strength class early? Please leave your weights. We’ll put them away, so the room can stay quiet for everyone still practicing.
  6. Membership cancellation is by email. Email hello@ignyteyoga.com to request it. The note needs to be in 30 days before your next billing cycle. Inside that window, the next charge can still go through.

In the room

Classes are 60 minutes unless the class description says otherwise. Wear comfortable athletic clothes and bring a mat, a sweat towel, and any props you already have, such as blocks and straps. The studio has kettlebells and dumbbells for the strength and high-intensity classes. If you forget something, mats, blocks, water bottles, and apparel are available at the studio.

  • Dress lightly. A tank and light pants or shorts, or a fitted shirt and shorts, lets heat move instead of trapping it against you.
  • Drink water before, during, and after class.
  • Skip a full stomach. Try not to eat in the hour before class, and don’t come on an empty one either.
  • Listen to your body. Rest when you need to, and notice if the heat has reached your limit.
