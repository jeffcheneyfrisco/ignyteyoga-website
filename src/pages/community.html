Community Beyond the Walls

The practice keeps going after you leave the room.

Ignyte is the heated room on Dallas Parkway, and it is also the people who practice there. Sometimes that community steps outside together. These gatherings stay simple. Follow along when you want to join the next one.

Follow @ignyteyoga for upcoming dates.

Lake Grapevine evenings

A Lake Grapevine community evening is time outside with people from the studio. The gathering is yoga, a hike, and play outdoors at the lake. Come see the same faces you know from class, with more room to move and more sky overhead.

The first Ignyte Triathlon

The first Ignyte Triathlon brings three pieces of the practice into one gathering: Ignyte Sculpt 45 (Strength 45), power yoga, and a 5K. It is strength, flow, and a run, shared with the people you already practice beside.

Sunrise walks

Sunrise walks start at the studio and end at the studio. Step out with the community while the morning is quiet, then come back through the same door you left from.
