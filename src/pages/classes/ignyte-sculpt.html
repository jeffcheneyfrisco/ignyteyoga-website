IGNYTESCULPT

Move with strength. Flow with intention. Ignyte from within.

IGNYTESCULPT is our signature 60-minute class—a powerful fusion of yoga, strength, cardio, and core designed to challenge both body and mind.

Move through dynamic yoga flows layered with resistance training, light-to-moderate weights, cardio bursts, plyometrics, and core work. Expect higher repetitions, intentional transitions, and moments that challenge your endurance while still bringing you back to the breath, the movement, and the power you already carry within.

This class is energetic, empowering, and uniquely Ignyte. Come ready to move, sweat, challenge yourself, and discover what happens when strength and flow come together.

Class is heated to approximately 90°F using infrared heat panels.
