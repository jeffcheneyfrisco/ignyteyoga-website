STRENGTH45 | UPPER BODY

45 minutes. Focused strength. Maximum impact.

STRENGTH45 | UPPER BODY is a focused 45-minute strength class designed to maximize your time in the studio. We’ll start with a short, powered-up warm-up before moving into intentional strength training focused on the upper body and core.

Expect heavier weights, purposeful movement, and strength-based programming targeting the arms, shoulders, chest, back, and core. Fewer transitions. Focused work. Just 45 minutes to get strong, feel energized, and take on your day.

All classes take place in Infrared Heat.
