Deep Surrender

45 minutes to reset.

Deep Surrender is a gentle style of yoga that emphasizes body awareness with the coordination of movement and breath. This practice may incorporate extended periods of holding while also learning to surrender into your mind, body, and soul. This practice brings a little yin and yang back into your life, on and off the mat. 45 minutes to reset.

Suitable for all levels. No experience is necessary.
