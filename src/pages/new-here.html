New here?

And wondering if it’s right for you? We get it. It’s normal to think you have to be flexible, strong, or experienced to try yoga. But this couldn’t be farther from the truth. It’s called a yoga “practice” not a yoga “perfect” for a reason. We’ve all had ups and downs in our own practice. Trials and tribulations. But the benefits far outweigh the speed bumps. And each one of us started in our own way and we’ve grown in our individual practices on our own time. With each class, we promise that you’ll get more comfortable and see the benefits that yoga can bring into your life. The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.

Book a class

1. Before you come

Reserve a spot on the Mindbody schedule. Space is limited. If you can’t make a class, cancel at least 12 hours ahead so someone else can take it. Otherwise the studio charges a $15 no-show fee to the card on file.

HotFlo is a great place to start if you’re new to infrared heat and hot yoga. BlissFlo45 and Stretch are also for all levels, and no experience is necessary. PowerFlo is open to all levels, but some experience will be helpful. IgnyteSculpt and HotHIIT ask that you take at least two HotFlo or PowerFlo classes before attending. See every class.

Wear comfortable athletic wear. Bring your own yoga mat, sweat towel, and any props you have, such as blocks and straps. The studio provides kettlebells and dumbbells for the high-impact classes.

Dress lightly. Women may want a tank top and light yoga pants or shorts. Men may want a fitted t-shirt and shorts. Heavy clothing traps heat against your body.

Drink fluids before class. Try not to eat in the hour before class, and don’t come on an empty stomach either.

The studio is open daily, 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM, at 12021 Dallas Pkwy, Suite 300, Frisco, TX 75034.

2. Arriving

Please arrive 15 minutes early and plan to stay for the duration of the class. Arriving early gives your body time to adapt to the heat.

If you forget something, the studio has yoga mats, blocks, water bottles, and apparel available.

3. In the room

Classes are taught in a small, intimate setting under infrared heat lamps. A teacher leads the class. The panels warm you, your mat, and other objects in the room, evenly and silently. That is different from a forced-air room, which heats the air and can feel stuffy.

Classes are 60 minutes unless the description says otherwise. BlissFlo45 is 45 minutes. IgnyteSculpt45 is the shorter version of IgnyteSculpt. Stretch, PowerFlo, IgnyteSculpt, and HotHIIT are heated to 90°F.

Pay attention to your body. Rest if you need to, and stay alert to the signs that you may have reached your level of heat tolerance. Drink fluids during class.

The full list is on the policies page.

4. After class

Drink plenty of fluids after your session.

Questions

Do I need to be flexible?

No. The studio’s welcome says you do not have to be flexible, strong, or experienced to try yoga.

Which class should I take first?

HotFlo is described as a great place to start. BlissFlo45 and Stretch need no experience. PowerFlo is easier with some experience. IgnyteSculpt and HotHIIT come after at least two HotFlo or PowerFlo classes.

How long is class, and what if I need to cancel?

Classes are 60 minutes unless the description says otherwise. Cancel at least 12 hours ahead. A missed class without that notice is a $15 no-show fee.
