POWERFLO

Move with breath. Flow with intention. Find your power.

POWERFLO is a dynamic 60-minute power vinyasa class designed to build strength, endurance, flexibility, and connection through movement.

You’ll move through intentional, breath-led sequences that link one shape to the next, creating a powerful flow that challenges both body and mind. Expect opportunities to build heat, explore your edge, find moments of strength and stillness, and make the practice your own.

POWERFLO is strong, energizing, and rooted in the practice of yoga—inviting you to move beyond simply doing the poses and into experiencing the power of breath, movement, and presence.

Class is heated to approximately 90°F using infrared heat panels.

Breathe. Move. Find your power.

FLO

Slow down. Go deeper. Stay with it.

FLO is a 60-minute heated yoga class that invites you to slow the pace, deepen your awareness, and move with intention.

With longer holds, thoughtful transitions, and time to explore each shape, FLO creates space to build strength, mobility, balance, and connection to your breath. The slower pace allows you to notice more—how you move, where you hold tension, and what happens when you stay present through challenge.

Don’t mistake slower for easier. Expect moments of deep muscular engagement balanced with opportunities to soften, breathe, and create space within the body.

FLO is where strength meets stillness and effort meets awareness—giving you the space to experience your practice rather than rush through it.

Class is heated to approximately 90°F using infrared heat panels.

Slow the pace. Deepen the practice.

What is LYT?

Lara Heimann is an international yoga pioneer and Physical Therapist focused on evolving the practice of yoga to empower movement and balance amidst a modern lifestyle of technology and sedentariness. She is redefining the modern practice of yoga through her comprehensive LYT Yoga Method®, emphasizing alignment, functional movement, and spiritual wellness.

The LYT approach

Designed for your body’s needs, the LYT Method optimizes your body’s ability to move freely and efficiently. A fusion of yoga, physical therapy, and functional movement, it’s tailored to enhance functional movement, and core strength, and provide pain relief.

For more info regarding this methodology: https://lytyoga.com

HOTHIIT

You are STRONG! Be ready to WORK with an infrared heated HIIT workout.

High-intensity interval training (HIIT) is a training technique alternating short periods of intense or explosive exercises with brief recovery periods until the point of exhaustion. A HIIT workout has many benefits such as: high calorie burn, higher metabolic rate hours after the workout, helps burn fat and gain muscle, and improves endurance! Be prepared to push yourself and find your strength!

STRENGTH AND MOBILITY

Build strength. Create space. Move better.

STRENGTH & MOBILITY is a 60-minute class designed to build functional strength while improving mobility, stability, and range of motion.

Using heavier weights, controlled movements, and an intentional pace, you’ll slow things down and focus on how your body moves. Strength-building exercises are paired with purposeful mobility work to support your muscles and joints, improve movement quality, and build strength you can carry with you beyond the studio.

Expect less emphasis on speed and more focus on control, form, stability, and intentional movement. This class is approachable, challenging, and designed to help you feel strong and capable in your body.

Class is gently heated to approximately 85–89°F, creating a warm environment that supports movement without the heat becoming the challenge.

Move with intention. Build strength that lasts.

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced fitness enthusiast, our STRENGTH AND MOBILITY class offers a supportive and inclusive environment for everyone. STRENGTH AND FLO

Lift heavy. Move intentionally. Find your flow.

STRENGTH & FLO blends intentional strength training with the mobility, breath, and movement of yoga. Unlike our Sculpt classes, which typically use lighter weights and higher repetitions, STRENGTH & FLO encourages you to reach for heavier weights, slow things down, and work through fewer, more intentional reps.

Strength-focused blocks are broken up with yoga flow sequences designed to help you reset, move, breathe, and shake it out before picking the weights back up. Instead of rest, you will be guided through yoga sequences as your reset.

Expect functional strength training, controlled movement, heavier lifting, and several feel-good flows woven throughout class.

Less reps. More load. Intentional movement. And plenty of flow.

As always, you choose the weights and intensity that work for your body.
